About

I create immersive experiences

I'm a creative director and experience designer based in London. My work spans mixed reality installations, immersive theatre, live music experiences and industrial R&D.

I'm a Creative Technologist at Digital Catapult, the UK's advanced digital technology innovation centre, and co-founder and Creative Director of The Harbourers, an artist-led immersive theatre and production studio. My background in computer science and mathematics means I can take an idea from concept through technical architecture to delivery.
