01 · Royal Opera House · Engender

Immersive technology workshop for opera professionals

Context

Engender is The Royal Opera's initiative to address gender imbalance in opera and music theatre. Its network of women and non-binary opera professionals wanted access to immersive technologies, and its members came with very different levels of technical knowledge.

My role

I co-planned and co-led the opening workshop at Digital Catapult's immersive lab: an introduction to the technology and what's happening in the sector, with live demos, followed by hands-on time with XR kit. I worked directly with participants, advising on how immersive technology could serve their own creative ideas.

Delivered with Digital Catapult

02 · AI Guardian · SXSW 2024

Research workshops on ethical AI and digital identity

Context

Digital Catapult's investigation into ethical AI in the creative industries, developed with Target3D and Hat Trick Productions, and presented as an interactive MetaHuman experience at SXSW 2024.

My role

Ahead of the technical sprint, I designed and ran exploratory workshops using hyper-realistic digital characters to examine digital identity, representation and presence, helping set the visual and experiential language of the project.

I then researched the real-world tensions around ethical AI in creative work. My findings became training and guidance materials published alongside the experience, helping organisations engage critically and responsibly with AI.
