The Crash!

The brief

THE CRASH! begins with a man drifting through life, faithless and directionless, until a spaceship crash-lands in his living room. From the wreckage a woman alien emerges, and through their relationship, meaning re-enters the world. The album gestures at this story without stating it. My role was to make the allegory inhabitable, and place the audience inside it.

The structure

The evening combined a live podcast Q&A and a live performance. Rather than separate segments, I conceived them as two phases of one theatrical journey.

Act I

The Living Room

The Q&A takes place inside a fully staged living room: the before. As it closes, a crash is staged and the audience is rushed out, mirroring the character's rupture.

Act II

The Crash

The audience returns to a transformed room. Projection floods the walls and a movement artist embodies the alien, as visuals shift from cold and colourless to saturated and alive with each song.

Process

To brief the projection and movement artists precisely, I designed a structured prompt sheet for Steven: questions ranging from themes of liminal space, faith and connection to how people physically move through this world.

From his answers I built a world-building framework that became the shared compass for all three collaborators: a two-state colour logic, blue as the colour of meaning, the Romeo-and-Juliet shape of the central relationship, and the principle that the alien should be felt rather than literally depicted.
